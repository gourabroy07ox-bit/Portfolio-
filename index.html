Fitness tips

Small habits, steady results

Tip

Warm up for five minutes before any session — a light pulse raise lowers injury risk and improves performance.

Tip

Drink water before you feel thirsty. By the time thirst hits, you're already mildly dehydrated.

Tip

Sleep is part of training. Most adults recover best with seven to nine hours a night.

Tip

Progress isn't always visible day to day. Track weekly trends instead of daily fluctuations.

Tip

Mix cardio with strength work — each supports the other and lowers overall injury risk.

Tip

Rest days aren't wasted days. Muscle repair happens when you're not training.
